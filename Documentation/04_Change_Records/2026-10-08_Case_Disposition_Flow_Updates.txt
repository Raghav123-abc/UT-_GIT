CHANGE SUMMARY
--------------
Added three entry conditions on the Case Status field (Status_Filtered__c):
  1. Added filter: Case Status Equals 'New'
  2. Added filter: Case Status Equals 'Assigned'
  3. Added filter: Case Status Equals 'In Progress'

The three new filters are joined with OR, because a case has only one
status at a time. The existing five conditions are unchanged.
